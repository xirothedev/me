# Portfolio

A personal site. The site presents the author's work, writing, and tools through a small set of page types and a shared reading layout.

## Language

**Page**:
A published route on the site. Every Page is one of Home, Note, Blog, or Special page.
_Avoid_: Screen, view, document

**Home**:
The index Page. It introduces the author, lists Work, Notes, and Blogs, and offers the biography.
_Avoid_: Landing page, index

**Hero**:
The illustration beside the Home introduction. It shows a tropical beach, a palm park, and a city street.
_Avoid_: Painting, banner, cover image

**Work entry**:
One row in the Home Work list. It names a platform or open-source project, its kind, a one-line summary, and a status.
_Avoid_: Project card, portfolio item

**Note**:
An undated Page about an idea or practice. Notes are listed under "Notes" on Home.
_Avoid_: Essay, post, article

**Blog**:
A dated Page about an event or lesson. Blogs are listed under "Blogs" on Home.
_Avoid_: Post, entry, article

**Article**:
The reading layout shared by a Note or Blog: title, author line, body, and margin notes.
_Avoid_: Post, entry, document

**Special page**:
A Page with a layout of its own instead of the Article layout. The Special pages are Home, Hospital, Bookmarks, and Lab.
_Avoid_: Custom page, microsite, one-off

**Case study**:
The Hospital Special page. It describes the constraint, the stack, the failure modes, and what the author owns.
_Avoid_: Portfolio piece, project page

**Margin note**:
A short note attached to a passage. It appears beside the passage on wide screens and after the passage on narrow screens.
_Avoid_: Footnote, sidenote, annotation

**Bio mode**:
One of the two biography lengths on Home: Default or Long.
_Avoid_: Bio tab, short bio, about text

**Figure**:
An interactive explainer inside a Note or Blog that shows a process over time.
_Avoid_: Visualization, animation, diagram, widget

**Gallery**:
A set of images that opens in a Viewer.
_Avoid_: Grid, photo wall

**Viewer**:
The full-screen image overlay opened from a Gallery.
_Avoid_: Lightbox, modal

**Lab**:
A hidden Special page with small Tools. It is not listed on Home and is excluded from the sitemap.
_Avoid_: Playground, experiments page

**Tool**:
A client-side utility in the Lab. The current Tools are the Tuner, the Token estimator, and the Contrast checker.
_Avoid_: Widget, app, experiment

**Tuner**:
A Tool that listens to an instrument and shows the nearest note and the distance from it in cents.
_Avoid_: Pitch pipe, metronome
