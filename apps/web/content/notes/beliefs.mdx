<Heading id="shipping-fast-beats-the-best-strategy" level={2}>
  {'Shipping fast beats the best strategy'}
</Heading>

- [Speed](https://patrickcollison.com/fast) is a superpower
- Create a bias toward shipping
- Small teams ship faster
- Landings &gt; launches (i.e. product adoption &gt; shipping code)
- Listen, build, ship, tell the customer, then repeat forever

<Heading id="you-have-no-career-ceiling" level={2}>
  {'You have no career ceiling'}
</Heading>

- Grit &gt; talent
- There’s no substitute for [putting in the hours](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RDyg_41QF1w)
- Get [1% better](https://x.com/leerob/status/1841226051554308229) every day
- Do it yourself, don't wait for someone else
- Being helpful compounds

<Heading id="be-ruthlessly-truth-seeking" level={2}>
  {'Be ruthlessly truth-seeking'}
</Heading>

- The truth can be painful
- You can just change your mind if wrong
- Have strong opinions, loosely held
- Maximize your [exposure hours](https://articles.centercentre.com/user_exposure_hours/#:~:text=Exposure%20hours.,the%20designs%20that%20team%20produces)
- "Anecdata" &gt; data
- Seek the collective truth, not just [one opinion](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XY_problem#:~:text=The%20XY%20problem%20is%20a,(Y%20or%20Why%3F)>)

<Heading id="communication-is-the-job" level={2}>
  {'Communication is the job'}
</Heading>

- [Clear writing](https://paulgraham.com/talk.html) is clear thinking
- Everyone (yes, you) needs to become a [better writer](https://sorfis.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Writing-Tips-Scott-Adams-DFW-Sowin-compilation-Zweig.pdf)
- Leaders step up to provide clarity when it’s missing
- Be the person taking notes, even if it’s just for yourself
- Mismatched expectations lead to sadness
- Slack pro tip: anticipate objections before hitting send, then [address them](https://newsletter.weskao.com/p/super-specific-feedback)

<Heading id="leadership-doesnt-require-fancy-titles" level={2}>
  {"Leadership doesn't require fancy titles"}
</Heading>

- Influence &gt; titles
- Leaders do the work themselves _and_ delegate
- You can write your own playbook
- Study what worked for others, then take your own path

<Heading id="work-can-also-be-your-hobby" level={2}>
  {'Work can also be your hobby'}
</Heading>

- This doesn’t mean you can’t have other hobbies
- Passion + boundaries &gt; mythical "work-life balance"
- Your best work comes from [following your curiosity](https://x.com/collision/status/1529452415346302976)
- It's hard to beat someone obsessed who is having fun

<Heading id="hiring-should-feel-like-chewing-glass" level={2}>
  {'Hiring should feel like chewing glass'}
</Heading>

- There are two hiring answers: [hell yes](https://sive.rs/n) or no
- Growth potential &gt; current skill
- Hire people you can learn from
- Hire people you would someday be happy working for
- Favorite interview question: what work are you most proud of?

<Heading id="always-try-to-assume-good-intent" level={2}>
  {'Always try to assume good intent'}
</Heading>

- Lead with empathy
- They might just be having a bad day
- Criticism is [good feedback](/moderation#feedback) if you listen unemotionally

<Heading id="demos-memos" level={2}>
  {'Demos > memos'}
</Heading>

- You could have built a prototype during the meeting
- Only ship things you're [excited about yourself](https://x.com/rauchg/status/1932126547609334017)
